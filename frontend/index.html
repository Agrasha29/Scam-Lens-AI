STAY PROTECTED

Pause. Verify. Then Act.

A moment of verification can protect you from financial loss and identity theft.

  • Never share your OTP, UPI PIN or passwords.
  • Never click links in unexpected messages.
  • Verify payment requests through official channels.
  • Don't trust messages just because they look official.
